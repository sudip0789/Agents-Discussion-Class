What you’ll build in Part 2

A legal research agent you start with one command, /brief, followed by a court opinion in your cases/ folder. It:

  • asks your level, your purpose and how long the brief should be,
  • briefs the opinion, with an exact quote and a pinpoint cite for every claim,
  • keeps track of who is speaking: counsel, or the Court,
  • checks every claim and every cited case with a second, separate agent, and
  • says plainly that it didn’t research whether the case is still good law.

Then you try to break it, lock it so it can’t change its own rules, and switch its citation check from web search to CourtListener’s case-law database.

What you’ll type
/brief cases/marbury-v-madison.pdf

By the end of class you’ll be able to

  1. Name the parts behind every AI product: a model, instructions, tools, and a loop.
  2. Decide what an agent may access, and enforce it with permissions, not just polite instructions.
  3. Verify an agent’s work instead of trusting its summary.
  4. Use Git to see exactly how an agent changed over time.
